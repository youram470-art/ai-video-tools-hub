AI video tools hub

Three browser-based tools, described by what they are actually good at. No install, no affiliate links.

single shot

Wan 3.0

Text-to-video, image-to-video and motion control with presets. The camera-move presets are the reason to pick it: a slow push-in and a handheld drift read completely differently, and both are one click away.

wan-3.io

full pipeline

Pavo AI

Generation, timeline editing, captions and multilingual dubbing in one workspace. The feature that keeps me there is character consistency between shots — lock the reference frame once and subsequent shots stop drifting.

pavoai.app · prompt gallery

reference-driven

Rea Video

Video recreation: hand it a clip you like, describe the new subject, and it re-creates the shot. The shortest route from "that ad looked good" to "that ad with my product".

reavideo.pro
